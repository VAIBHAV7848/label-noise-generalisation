# Analytical Observations Protocol (Phase 0 Scaffold)

This document establishes the structured analytical template used to record, categorize, and cross-validate empirical observations during subsequent experimental phases.

---

## 1. Structured Observation Recording Standard

For every key empirical finding observed during experimental execution:
- **Observation ID**: Unique identifier (e.g. `OBS-001`).
- **Context & Regimes**: Exact dataset, architecture, noise type, noise rate $\eta$, and random seed set.
- **Empirical Measurement**: Exact quantitative metric delta ($\Delta$ Accuracy, $\Delta$ ECE, $\Delta \|\hat{T}-T\|_F$).
- **Consistency Check**: Did this observation replicate across all 5 seeds? (Yes/No with $p$-value).
- **Hypothesis Link**: Which hypothesis (H1, H2, H3, H4) does this observation support or refute?

---

## 2. Preliminary Literature-Derived Baseline Expectations

Based on established theoretical literature (Arpit et al. 2017, Zhang & Sabuncu 2018, Charoenphakdee et al. 2019, Wei et al. 2022):

1. **Expectation 1 (Memorisation Inflexion)**: In overparameterized models (PreAct-ResNet18), clean validation accuracy will reach a peak during early epochs (epochs 15–35) before degrading as noisy labels are memorized. In underparameterized models (Logistic Regression), validation accuracy will plateau smoothly without an early peak.
2. **Expectation 2 (Loss Correction Sensitivity)**: Backward correction will show higher test accuracy variance and occasional gradient instability compared to Forward correction when $\eta \ge 0.4$, due to negative loss values generated by $T^{-1}$.
3. **Expectation 3 (Calibration Degradation)**: GCE and SCE will achieve high test classification accuracy on symmetric noise, but their Expected Calibration Error (ECE) will be significantly higher than clean baselines due to probability downweighting.
4. **Expectation 4 (Synthetic-to-Real Transfer Drop)**: Anchor-point-based loss correction will experience a significant drop in relative ranking when transitioning from synthetic CIFAR-10 pair-flip noise to CIFAR-10N human noise.
