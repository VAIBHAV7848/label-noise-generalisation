| **CLM-01**: Backward/Forward loss correction is unbiased under known $T$. | Theory | Prop 1 (Patrini 2017) | Known literature standard. | **TRIVIAL/KNOWN**. This is not a novel claim; it is a restatement of prior art. | "As established by Patrini et al., loss correction yields unbiased risk..." |
| **CLM-02**: Excess risk scales with $\|T^{-1}\|_2^2 \|\hat{T} - T\|_F$. | Theory | Prop 2A / 2B | Proven under independent $\hat{T}$. | **DISCONNECTED**. Empirical implementation (Confident Learning) uses same-sample estimation, violating the independence assumption of the proof. | "Assuming independent estimation of $T$, we bound..." |
| **CLM-03**: Transition-matrix ill-conditioning drives severe empirical degradation. | Empirical | Pilot Table 1 & Table 3 (Asym 40%). | Confident Learning yields $\kappa = 34.66$, accuracy drops. | **SUPPORTED**. Strong empirical link between $\kappa$ and performance collapse. | "We observe that empirical matrix ill-conditioning severely degrades accuracy..." |
| **CLM-04**: GCE outperforms True $T$ on Symmetric Noise. | Empirical | Pilot Table 1 (Sym 50%: GCE 82.40% vs TrueT 79.17%). | $N=3$ paired $t$-test. | **CONTRADICTORY**. If True $T$ is the optimal unbiased estimator, why does a heuristic beat it? The paper fails to explain this paradox. | "Empirically, bounded losses like GCE can outperform exact matrix inversion under high symmetric noise, likely due to favorable gradient regularization." |
| **CLM-05**: Overparameterized models exhibit early learning then memorisation decay. | Empirical | Figure 9 (Validation dynamics). | Supported by Arpit 2017. | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**. Confirmed for ResNet-18, but claims about "capacity scaling" require testing varying parameter counts. | "For PreActResNet-18, we observe an early generalisation peak..." |
| **CLM-06**: Label noise inherently induces probability miscalibration. | Theory | Theorem 4A / 4B | Proven for symmetric noise. | **OVERSTATED**. The exact ECE bound is only proven for symmetric noise, but the text implies it is universal. | "Symmetric label noise provably induces a strict calibration penalty..." |
| **CLM-07**: Corrupted validation sets trap Temperature Scaling, degrading ECE. | Empirical | Table 2 ($\Delta$ECE up to +16.63%). | $N=3$ paired $t$-test. | **SUPPORTED BUT NAIVE**. Standard TS assumes clean labels. Tuning it on noisy labels is a strawman. | "Applying standard Temperature Scaling to corrupted validation sets severely degrades test calibration." |
| **CLM-08**: GCE preserves intrinsic calibration without validation tuning. | Empirical | Table 2 (GCE ECE = 0.0909 in Sym 50%). | $N=3$ paired $t$-test. | **SUPPORTED**. | "In-training robust losses like GCE maintain stable uncalibrated confidence." |
